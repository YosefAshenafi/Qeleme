# Qelem – Google Site Setup Guide (for Google Play)

Google Play requires a website. Google suggests **Google Sites** (sites.google.com) as an easy option. Follow these steps to create a basic, compliant site.

---

## Step 1: Create the site

1. Go to **https://sites.google.com**
2. Sign in with the same Google account you use for Google Play (or your developer account).
3. Click **Blank** or **Create** to start a new site.
4. Name the site: **Qelem** (or “Qelem – Educational App”).

---

## Step 2: Add the content (copy-paste below)

Use the **Insert** panel on the right to add sections. For each section, add a **Text** or **Title + Text** block and paste the content.

---

### Section 1 – Hero / Title (top of page)

**Title (large):**
```
Qelem
```

**Subtitle / tagline:**
```
Educational platform for interactive learning
```

**Short line (optional):**
```
MCQs, flashcards, and progress tracking for students.
```

### Section 2 – About Qelem

**Heading:** About Qelem

**Body:**

```
Qelem is an educational app designed to help students learn through interactive content. It offers multiple choice questions (MCQ), flashcards, and progress tracking for different grades and subjects, including kindergarten (KG) content.

The app supports English and Amharic and provides a secure login, profiles, and optional premium features.
```

## Step 3: Add your app icon (optional)

1. In your project, use: `assets/images/logo/app-icon.png`
2. In Google Sites: **Insert → Image → Upload** and select that file.
3. Place it next to the “Qelem” title at the top.

---

## Step 4: Publish and get your URL

1. Click **Publish** (top right).
2. Choose **Web address**.
3. Enter a URL, e.g. `qelem` or `qelem-app`.
4. Your site URL will be: **https://sites.google.com/view/qelem** (or whatever you chose).
5. Click **Publish**.

---

## Step 5: Use the URL in Google Play

1. Open **Google Play Console** → your app **Qelem**.
2. Go to **Store presence** (or **Main store listing**).
3. In **Website** (or **Developer website**), paste your Google Site URL, e.g.:
   `https://sites.google.com/view/qelem`
4. Save.
